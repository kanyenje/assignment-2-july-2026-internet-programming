Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

1931 - 2015

"Dream, Dream, Dream. Dreams transform into thoughts and thoughts result in action."

The Missile Man of India

Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam was an Indian aerospace scientist and statesman who served as the 11th President of India from 2002 to 2007. He was born on October 15, 1931, in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, and raised in a humble background.

Kalam worked as a scientist and science administrator, spending four decades at the Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO) and the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO). He played a pivotal role in India's civilian space programme and military missile development, earning him the nickname "Missile Man of India."

Key Achievements

🚀 SLV-3 Project

Led India's first indigenous satellite launch vehicle project, successfully launching the Rohini satellite into orbit in 1980.

🛡️ Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme

Developed key missiles including Agni, Prithvi, Trishul, Nag, and Akash under this program.

🏛️ President of India

Served as the 11th President of India from 2002 to 2007, known as the "People's President."

📚 Author & Educator

Authored several books including "Wings of Fire," "India 2020," and "Ignited Minds," inspiring millions of young Indians.

🏅 Bharat Ratna

Received India's highest civilian award, the Bharat Ratna, in 1997 for his contributions to science and nation-building.

🌱 Youth Icon

Worked extensively to inspire youth, encouraging them to pursue science and contribute to India's development.

Legacy

Dr. Kalam's legacy continues to inspire generations. His vision for a developed India, his dedication to science and education, and his humble personality have left an indelible mark on the nation. He is remembered not only as a great scientist and president but also as a teacher who never stopped learning and teaching.

Several institutions, awards, and initiatives have been named after him, including the Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam International Foundation and the Kalam-Kalpana Chawla Memorial Award.
